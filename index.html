Karta × Bridge Karta Stablecoin via Open Issuance 12 / 13

What we're building

Karta-branded stablecoin issued via Bridge Open Issuance. Becomes the default store-of-value inside Karta — every user balance auto-converts to it. Self-custody preserved. Transparent reserves. Programmable by design.

· Self-custody preserved
· Transparent reserves
· Programmable by design
Target by EOY 2026

$20M in reserves

Current GTV trajectory supports this. Reserve yield ~3–4%.

Win for Bridge
  • Reserve volume scales directly with our GTV growth
  • Distribution via TMA + iOS/Android apps
  • Co-marketing launch (joint press, public announcement)
  • Pilot for future Bridge stablecoin products
Win for Karta
  • Revenue via reserve yield
  • Native asset — UX consistency, lower frictions
  • Strategic alignment with Bridge long-term
Win for users
  • Native stablecoin — no bridging, no slippage
  • Self-custody preserved
  • Transparent proof of reserves
The ask

Kick off Open Issuance commercial discussion + technical onboarding sequence.

Tempo — Stripe's stablecoin infra — aligned with Bridge as issuance partner. We're moving in the same direction.
